ABOUT ME

ALEX CEE

I started DJing because of my passion for music and making people dance. I love bringing energy to a party and creating those moments where everyone is on the dance floor enjoying themselves.

As a versatile DJ, I enjoy playing everything from Latin and Reggaeton to Hip-Hop and House. I also like connecting with my clients on a personal level, getting to know them and the atmosphere they want to create so it feels like more than just showing up and playing music.
